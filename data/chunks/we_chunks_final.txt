Investor Relations CSR Other Services ( Telecom Egypt Home WE Mobile Packages ) OTHERS SERVICES MOBILE CALL SERVICES ‹ › WE Pay WE Pay is a digital wallet that enables you to control your financial transactions. You can transfer money, cash in & out, pay WE bills, utility bills (electricity, water, gas), send donations and other services easily and securely. How to subscribe to WE Pay Visit the nearest WE store with your valid national ID and SIM card registered under your name. Fill in the subscription form and sign it. Download and install WE Pay app from: Open the app and choose Activate Account. Fill in your data including (Full Name – National ID No – Active WE Mobile Number), and accept the terms and conditions. An OTP SMS will be sent to your mobile phone to activate the App. Create a new six-digit PIN number (do not use consecutive or repeated digits, such as 5555). This Pin will be used in all transactions performed via the App. Load the wallet and enjoy WE Pay services.
